# Magno Skin Care Clinic

## About Us

Welcome to **Magno Skin Care Clinic**, your trusted partner in achieving healthy, radiant skin. Conveniently located in Barangay Malinao, Pasig City, we provide comprehensive and personalized dermatological care for all your skin, hair, and nail needs. 

Whether you are seeking effective medical treatments for stubborn skin conditions or advanced aesthetic procedures to rejuvenate your appearance, our clinic is dedicated to delivering safe, evidence-based, and results-driven care. At Magno Skin Care Clinic, we believe that healthy skin is the foundation of confidence, and we are here to help you look and feel your absolute best.

---

## Our Services

### Medical Dermatology
*Expert diagnosis and treatment for a wide range of skin, hair, and nail conditions.*

*   **Acne Management:** Comprehensive acne and pimple treatments, including targeted pimple injections.
*   **Fungal Infections:** Effective solutions for *buni* (ringworm), *had-had* (jock itch), and *alipunga* (athlete's foot).
*   **Skin Conditions:** Management of Eczema, Skin Allergies, and Psoriasis.
*   **Pigmentation & Coloration:** Treatments for Melasma and improvement of Vitiligo.
*   **Hair & Nail Care:** Falling hair treatments and medical care for nail infections.
*   **Minor Procedures:** Keloid injections and Electrocautery for the safe removal of warts, syringoma, and milia.

### Aesthetic & Cosmetic Services
*Advanced treatments designed to exfoliate, contour, and restore your skin's natural glow.*

*   **Facial Care:** Deep-cleansing facial treatments for a refreshed complexion.
*   **Exfoliation & Resurfacing:** Diamond Peeling and Chemical Peeling (available for both Face and Body).
*   **Laser Hair Reduction:** IPL (Intense Pulsed Light) treatments for underarms.
*   **Skin Tightening & Contouring:** RF (Radio Frequency) therapy to target fine lines, address sagging skin, and improve facial contour.
